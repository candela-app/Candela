# Rotatory Wheel — visual search and touch

Rotatory Wheel is a **moving-target visual search and visuomotor task**. It is not an alphabet test, reading test, saccade test, visual-acuity test, or neglect diagnostic.

Headline outputs: median correct-target response time (with IQR), clean-tap rate, discrimination-error rate, and motor-miss rate, under a locked device, speed, bubble count, cue mode, and hand.

## Session protocol

1. Click to start (not scored).
2. Three unscored warm-up trials.
3. Unique scored deck: 26 letters, 10 digits, or 24 color trials.
4. Device bubble count is locked: 2 phone / 3 tablet / 5 TV.
5. Speed, contrast, cue mode, and rotation direction stay fixed during the block.
6. A pause during an open trial keeps the raw taps and marks the trial invalid for RT summaries.
7. Midline onsets (|x| < 10% of the wheel) are a third field, not forced right.

## Results

- Parent card: median response time, clean taps, wrong-bubble rate, empty-wheel miss rate.
- Laterality only when each side has ≥10 valid onset trials, balance ≤ 2:1, and hand is recorded.
- Clinician CSV: trial-level onset/tap fields, errors, crowding proxy, eccentricity bin, quality flags.
- Do not compare raw scores across phone, tablet, and TV.
